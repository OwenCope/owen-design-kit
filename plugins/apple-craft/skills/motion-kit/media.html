Films have no autoplay attribute. The manager lazy-loads each source near the viewport, plays at most two at once (one on narrow screens), and pauses everything when the tab hides, on save-data, or under Reduce Motion. The scrub draws the nearest loaded frame and redraws only when the frame changes.
