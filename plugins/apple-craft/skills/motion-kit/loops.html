Loop parts

Four pieces of motion that run on their own, shown on Harbor, a fictional week planner for Mac. Each one starts paused or stops on its own, and each one answers to Reduce Motion.

Drift. Ambient layers breathe a few pixels behind the headline, never on text. Motion.drift(el)

Your week, planned on Sunday night.

Harbor lays out projects, habits and rest in one calm view, then keeps it where you left it.

Marquee. A constant speed text loop. Hover or tab in to pause it. Motion.marquee(el, { speed })
