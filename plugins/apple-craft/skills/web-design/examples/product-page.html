Questions, answered.

What does Harbor actually store?

Only the file location and the app that opened it. Your files stay where they are; Harbor never copies or uploads them.

Which apps work with Harbor?

Any Mac app that opens files: Keynote, Notes, Preview, Xcode, Figma and the rest. If it has a file on disk, Harbor can park it.

What happens when the beta ends?

Everything you parked stays yours and keeps working. Beta members keep a free year when Harbor launches this winter.

Do I need an account?

No. Harbor works entirely on your Mac with no sign-in, no sync and no tracking.
