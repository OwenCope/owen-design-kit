# Taste, written down

Taste is mostly a long list of small decisions made the same way every time. These are mine. They're starting points, not laws; when the product needs something else, the product wins, but I need a reason.

## What good feels like

- **One thing at a time.** One headline, one picture, one action per screen. When I find myself adding a second of anything, I ask which one I'd keep.
- **Calm, then one surprise.** Quiet type, generous space, real content, and exactly one moment that makes you stop. Two surprises cancel each other out.
- **It looks inevitable.** Good work looks like it couldn't have been any other way. If I can easily imagine five equally good layouts, I haven't found the idea yet.
- **The product is the hero.** Not a gradient, not an illustration, not a headline. The real thing, big and sharp.
- **Nothing is there to fill space.** Empty space is fine. Filler (stat rows, icon grids, testimonial carousels, "trusted by" walls with no reason) is not.

## What I reject on sight

- A headline bigger than the product it's about.
- Gradient text, purple-to-blue glows, glass on everything, emoji as icons.
- A grid of six identical cards with an icon, a title and a sentence. It says nothing.
- A drawn copy of something real: a fake app window, letter tiles instead of app icons, coloured squares instead of a Dock.
- Two buttons fighting for "primary".
- Anything that moves when you press it.
- A panel that appears empty and fills a moment later.
- Text that's 10 or 11 px to make it fit.
- A section that exists because "landing pages have one".

## Numbers I trust

- Controls animate in 200 to 350 ms; panels and morphs about 340 ms; section entrances 500 to 800 ms, once. Nothing linear, nothing bouncy on controls.
- Content follows a growing shape within about 30 ms; it never waits for the spring to settle.
- Body text 15 to 18 px at about 1.5 line height; headlines get tighter as they grow (about -0.02em at 48 px, -0.04em at 96 px).
- Tap targets 44 px. Text contrast 4.5:1, big headlines 3:1.
- Side padding that never lets content touch the edge; on desktop usually 24 to 48 px plus a max width.
- One accent colour. Greys from one hue.

## How I choose between two options

1. Which one shows the product more clearly? Usually that settles it.
2. Which one could only belong to this product? Prefer it.
3. Which one has fewer parts? Prefer it.
4. Which one will still look right with real, messy content? Prefer it.
5. If it's still a tie, it's taste: show Owen both and let him pick. Record what he picked in `picks.md`.

## Owen's taste specifically

- Apple-like, but alive: real Liquid Glass, real motion, not static Apple pastiche.
- He notices a 1 px movement on hover, a 4 px gap difference, a wallpaper that's too loud. Assume he'll notice.
- He likes his own products' identity (Gaze's face, SakuraCord's petals). Never trade it for something generic or borrowed.
- He reacts to feel more than to rules. "Not smooth", "slop", "...." are about how it feels; find the cause.
- He'd rather see an honest 5 and a plan than a dressed-up 7.
